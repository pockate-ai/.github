# Pokate Labs

**Infrastructure for agents using real computers.**

AI agents can reason about work. To complete useful tasks, they also need a trustworthy way to interact with files, applications, and devices. Pokate Labs builds the control, state, safety, and evaluation systems that let people delegate that work while staying informed and in charge.

Our mission is to make agent work on real computers useful, understandable, and safe to supervise. We focus on bounded capabilities, human authority, recoverable changes, privacy, and evidence that clearly distinguishes what an agent requested from what a host carried out and what could be independently verified.

## What we are building

| Control | State | Safety | Lab |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Runtime · Mandate · Arbiter | Passport · Twin · Atlas | Branch · Time Machine · Witness | Evaluation · Certification · Benchmarking |

**Pokate Companion** is the human control plane across the portfolio, organized around **Chat → Activity → Settings**. **Pokate Bridge** is a capability SDK that feeds compatible integrations into Atlas. The host machine remains the execution platform; supported host software enforces the permissions and policies for computer actions.

This is the target portfolio architecture. Runtime, Passport, and Arbiter have experimental implementations; Companion is in development preview. Other product lines and Windows/Linux adapters are planned. Product names and diagrams are not release or support claims. See the [current product status](https://github.com/pockate-ai/.github/blob/main/docs/product-status.md).

## Our goals

- Give agents only the typed, task-scoped capabilities they need.
- Keep people in control of sensitive or consequential actions.
- Carry useful task context between agents without transferring credentials or authority.
- Make supported changes reviewable and recoverable while preserving human work.
- Record honest, privacy-conscious evidence of actions and their outcomes.
- Evaluate products with repeatable methods, visible limits, and reported failures.
- Make each product useful and supportable on its own, with clear ownership and release evidence.
