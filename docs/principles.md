# Pokate Labs working principles

## Human control stays visible

People should understand what an agent is doing, what needs attention, and what happened. Consequential actions receive clear, exact review. People can deny or stop work and reach a useful recovery path.

## Access is narrow and enforced

Agents use typed, bounded tools on an enforcing host. Discovery, task continuity, coordination, and observation do not grant permission. Credentials and unrestricted computer access are not passed to agents.

## Local capability comes first

Security controls should remain useful on the host without a required hosted backend, relay, or phone. Optional integrations add coordination and convenience with their own contracts and privacy choices.

## Evidence describes what was actually proved

Separate agent claims, host records, independent observations, and verified outcomes. State the tested versions and conditions. Report skipped tests, missing coverage, failures, and unknown outcomes plainly.

## Recovery protects human work

Offer recovery only for effects with known support. Detect conflicts and preserve changes made by people after an agent's checkpoint. External effects may be irreversible or uncertain and need explicit reconciliation.

## Privacy is part of the product

Collect only what a user job requires. Scope and redact evidence, define retention, and support export and deletion. Machine data is not an unconsented benchmark dataset.

## Every product earns its place

A product needs a clear user job, independent adoption path, contract boundary, accountable human owner, privacy and retention policy, meaningful tests, support plan, and release evidence. A name or roadmap is not proof of delivery.

## Keep the human experience simple

The primary Companion flow is Chat → Activity → Settings. Expose machine and infrastructure detail progressively, under an advanced path when needed. Use touch-native interaction, accessibility, readable progress, and useful empty, offline, error, and success states.
