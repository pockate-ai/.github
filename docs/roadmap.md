# Pokate Labs roadmap direction

Pokate Labs is building the portfolio around evidence and dependencies rather than public launch dates. Product phases remain open until their acceptance conditions are met. This page gives the company-level sequence; it does not replace the acceptance gates for each product.

## 1. Establish the shared boundaries

Keep product names, maturity, owners, contracts, and source boundaries explicit. Define small compatible interfaces for tasks, capabilities, authority, resources, effects, evidence, and recovery. Preserve working products and established technical identities through tested migrations.

## 2. Prove the first independent workflows

Continue Runtime work on bounded execution, authority checks, durable jobs, stopping, and recovery. Advance Passport as a standalone way to capture, inspect, validate, and continue a task without transferring credentials or approval. Use Pokate Lab fixtures to evaluate both workflows and report failures and mandatory gaps.

## 3. Add supported recovery and developer integrations

Prove conflict-aware checkpoints and restoration for supported changes. Build Bridge against an enforcing host contract and require capability-specific conformance. Feed Witness only evidence with clear provenance, coverage, privacy, and retention.

## 4. Connect human authority and coordination

Project the same authoritative jobs through Companion. Preserve Chat → Activity → Settings, exact human review, offline honesty, cancellation, and recovery. Add Mandate and Arbiter when delegation, resource identity, and coordination have complete enforcing semantics and repeatable demand.

## 5. Expand with measured evidence

Develop Branch, Twin, and Atlas through their independent product gates. Release each qualifying product with a supported-version matrix, installation and update evidence, privacy and retention terms, recovery behavior, and named human ownership. Verify Mac, Windows, and Linux independently before making parity claims. Certification requires separate criteria, governance, expiry, and independent scrutiny.

## How progress is assessed

Measure repeated task and handoff use, context reconstruction, completion with independent verification, recovery without loss of human edits, approval friction, denied boundary attempts, compatibility failures, observation coverage, reproducibility, and paid demand. Publish the conditions, numerator and denominator, skipped cases, failures, and uncertainty. Do not compress different guarantees into one universal safety score.

## Timing and release claims

There are no committed public launch dates in this roadmap. Suggested discovery counts and rolling planning horizons are planning aids, not promises or measured adoption. A product or platform is described as released only when its own acceptance and release evidence exists.
